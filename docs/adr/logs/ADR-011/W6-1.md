# ADR-011 — W6-1: pack among the opening commands

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W6-1.** D16 item 3: pack declares
itself as opening a package, and the cli adapter's `openingCommands` gives
it `pack --json {package} --out packed.metais`, an authoring command line.
Every `security` vector's open step now runs through pack and gives
`ok: false` with the same findings and nothing written: pack opens before
it reads anything else of its operand or its output, and a packed hostile
package is opened, and refused by the open, before pack refuses a packed
operand, as unpack does (the stricter reading, confirmed by the plan
orchestrator at approval). This is W6-1's only edit to the runner.
