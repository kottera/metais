# ADR-011 — W7-E2: the §4.2 staged incident report, with SC2's test

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W7-E2.** `examples/dora-incident-report`
is the package of PRD §4.2, and Wave 6's harness, `TestExamplePackages`,
runs over it: check and test green, packed and green again, and previewed
with `--optional all` and `none` into output that validates (SC4).

- **The glossary is the schemas.** The package carries the data glossary of
  Annex II to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/302. Field 1.1, the type of
  report, is the `stage` input: `initial notification`, `intermediate
  report` or `final report`. The rest of section 1 is the `entity` input,
  and sections 2, 3 and 4 are the `incident` input. Every property is
  titled with its field's number and name, and carries the number as
  `x-field` and the glossary's type as `x-field-type`.
- **The stage decides the required fields.** The entity's and the
  incident's own schemas require the fields mandatory at every stage,
  sections 1 and 2. The package's `input_constraints` schema requires
  section 3's mandatory fields when the stage is the intermediate or the
  final report, and section 4's when it is the final report. This is the
  constraint shape of the `dat/input-constraints` vector. A conditional
  field whose condition the data shows, such as 3.24 when 3.23 includes
  other, is required by an `if`/`then`. Where the condition turns on a
  classification criterion of 2.5 and the field belongs to a later
  section, the stage's branch gates it.
- **Each stage's report amends the one before.** The three examples are
  one incident's initial notification, intermediate report and final
  report, each the previous one's inputs with the stage changed, fields
  added and section 2 corrected.
- **SC2's test** is `TestSC2IncidentReportStages` in
  `cmd/metais/sc2_test.go`. It builds the binary and validates, at each
  stage, four reports, each against a table written from the glossary's
  columns rather than from the schemas:
  - exactly the stage's mandatory fields, which is accepted;
  - none of them, which reports each field as `input.required`, and
    nothing else;
  - the previous stage's report unchanged, which reports exactly the
    fields the stage adds;
  - every condition met, which reports exactly the conditional fields the
    stage makes mandatory.

  It was seen red twice: first refused with
  `package.layout.missing_manifest` before the package existed, then,
  with the manifest's `input_constraints` removed, failing six subtests
  of the intermediate and final reports.

The sources are the EU's DORA texts, recorded in the package's
`references/sources.md` with their reuse terms. The package was built
before W7-A1's terms check ended, and does not merge before it, by the
plan orchestrator's ruling.
