# ADR-004 — W7-4: the log completed from the plan's wave verdicts — Wave 2's front end and dates, and Wave 3's renderer and filters

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W7-4.** Plan §8 to §11 name ADR-004
in Waves 2 to 5. This log recorded Waves 4 and 5, and Wave 3 only through
W3-5's `yaml` writer note. Wave 2's front end and dates, and Wave 3's
engine and standard filters, were recorded in ADR-010's log under D4,
not here. This entry records them under this ADR's decisions, from the
plan's verdicts and the waves' closeouts
([wave 2](../../../briefs/brief-plan-2026-10-metais-v1-wave2.md),
[wave 3](../../../briefs/brief-plan-2026-10-metais-v1-wave3.md)).

**Wave 2: D1 to D3 and D16 item 4, in the front end.**

- **W2-15 ([#38](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/38))** landed the
  lexer and the `strict2` parser, and `internal/liquid/ast` with spans and
  binding constructs marked. It also landed the dialect interfaces,
  `ParseValue`, `ParseCondition`, the `include` refusal and
  `template-nesting`.
- **Golden Liquid's parse cases:** 1,069 of 1,098 passed, with 29
  `dialect` exclusions. The 17 `include` cases cite D1 item 5. 97 cases
  that this ADR refuses at render, not at parse, were deferred to Wave 3
  in `render-time.txt`, each naming its item.
- **D11's maths:** W2-14 ([#36](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/36))
  landed `internal/dates`, with `ParseNow`, `FromValue` and the closed
  `strftime` set. It is tested under four `TZ` values in child processes,
  and years are bounded to 0000–9999, the stricter rule. Plan §8 ruled
  that `date` lives in `internal/dates` and that `internal/template`
  registers it through `Extend`. So `Core()` holds the rest of D10's
  standard set.

**Wave 3: D2 to D6 and D10's standard catalog without `date`, in the
engine.**

- **W3-3 ([#48](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/48))** landed
  `Render`, `Eval` and `EvalCondition`. It covers every D2 tag, `render`
  through the `Partials` port, the meter with ADR-009 D11 item 2's
  charges, panic recovery, ADR-010 A2's single iteration seam and
  `FuzzLiquidRender`.
- **W3-4 ([#46](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/46))** landed
  `liquid.Core()`: every standard filter but `date`, and `escape`'s alias
  `h` (ADR-010 A4). `Check` refuses a literal arity at parse, which moved
  73 cases out of `render-time.txt`.
- **Golden Liquid's render run** met W3-3 and W3-4. It held 1,098 cases: 10
  selected out for applying `date`, 1,088 run, 913 passing and 175 excluded,
  every exclusion a `dialect` line naming its item. Plan §9 ruled that the
  `date` cases were out of Wave 3's scope, not excluded. Wave 5's W5-1
  restored them.

**Waves 4 and 5** are recorded above (W4-3, W4-6, W5-1 to W5-5). The
delegated liquid-spec run of ADR-011 D17 measures this ADR's departures
from Shopify's engine, and ADR-011's log records its results.
