# ADR-004 — W2-1: Amendment A2's `money` alternative, the filters' help, and chapter 13 §13.9

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-values-and-rules W2-1.** Decision items
5, 6 and 8 are built. Chapter 8's MTS-FLT-014 wording, MTS-FLT-023 with its
Note, chapter 13's opening sentence and §13.9 landed as `b0cbb230` wrote
them. `template.unknown_filter`'s `alternative` parameter, its hint and its
statement were registered as `b0cbb230` wrote them, and the registry, the
specdata copies, `spec/statements.json` and chapter 12's code catalog were
regenerated.

- **The alternative (item 5).** `internal/template/filters` holds an
  explicit table, `money`, `money_with_currency` and
  `money_without_currency`, each to `amount`, and `Alternative` reads it.
  Both places that map the engine's `unknown_filter` to its finding, the
  template's parse (`internal/template/codes.go`) and a manifest
  expression's (`internal/loader/outputs.go`), give the parameter from it.
  No name gets one by its spelling's nearness: the vectors `flt/money`,
  `flt/money-with-currency` and `flt/money-without-currency` prove each
  name, and `flt/money-near-names` proves that `moneys`, `mony`, `Money`,
  `amounts`, `amout` and `money_with_currencies` get none.
- **Help (item 6).** `metais help templates` gives `date`, `number`,
  `amount` and `duration` a group of their own, each entry example first
  with chapter 8's example. `amount`'s entry ends with a line of its own
  saying there is no `money` filter and that `amount` takes the currency's
  major unit, not cents; `duration`'s says its directives mirror `date`'s.
  `TestTemplatesTopicValues` holds both, beside the golden.
- **Authoring (item 8).** `internal/template`'s
  `TestWritingValuesForReaders` reads §13.9's input, template and output
  from the chapter itself, renders the template in the package dialect and
  gets exactly the four lines the section says it writes.
