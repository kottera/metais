# ADR-013 — W2-11: the landing page and the two guides

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-public W2-11.** The site's front page
is D8 item 3's landing page, and "Using a package" and "Writing a package"
are pages under `/guide/` (D1 item 5): `/guide/using-a-package/` and
`/guide/writing-a-package/`. Their words are the drafts of #265, which Ed
approved in W2-OOB5, with his edits: the tagline (M2), the second
paragraph of "Who makes METAIS" (M3), and the invoice template's money
line, now the `amount` filter (M6). The drafts were converted once to
HTML by `internal/tools/site/mdhtml`, and the templates hold the result;
the markup around the words is the site's own: links from each command and
help topic named in the text to its reference page, the navigation, and
the guides' Liquid held in a `raw` block. Two quoted outputs of "Using a
package" were re-quoted from the binary built from `main`, which now
prints a `/status/components/*` row and whole enums in `schema --fields`,
and a longer `validate` hint.

`/getting-started/`, which the guides replace, redirects to
`/guide/using-a-package/` (M7), and the navigation links both guides in
its place. `site`'s `TestGettingStartedRedirect` holds the redirect, and
`TestInternalLinks` holds its target.

The guide's invoice ships as `examples/invoice` (M5), which
`internal/cli`'s `TestExamplePackages` checks, tests, packs and previews
like every example. `site`'s `TestWritingGuideInvoice` holds its schema and
template identical to the guide's blocks, and its manifest to the guide's
inputs and outputs; it was seen red with the example's template changed.
`TestTeraOnlyWhoMakes` holds the built site to D2 item 1: one section of
the landing page names Kottera's other product, and no other page does.
`TestPageCommands` takes one global flag alone, as in `metais --version`.
