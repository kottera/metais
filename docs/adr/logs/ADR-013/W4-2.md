# ADR-013 — W4-2: the v1.0.0 launch record

**2026-10-26, PLAN-2026-10-metais-public W4-2.** The launch record of
`v1.0.0` (D7 items 3 and 8), [`docs/dev/rehearsal/v1.0.0.md`](../../../dev/rehearsal/v1.0.0.md),
beside the candidates' records and in their format. It was written from
GitHub's API, from W4-OOB1's report and its log of `rehearse.yml`'s run,
and not from T-A3's report, which is outside this train. So the
release's own facts in it are GitHub's.

- **The release.** `v1.0.0` at `f7f57020`, `spec-1.0` at `6289d1b5`,
  published by `release.yml`'s run 40312151129 after the `release`
  environment's approval. The tags, the release, the tap's formula and
  the site's deploy were published in that order. An earlier release run,
  40310757343, was cancelled while it waited for approval, and published
  nothing.
- **The launch checks.** Under R-W4-1, the first run, 40312702820, failed
  three steps on a harness defect. It is recorded as the first run, with
  its mechanism and its repair: #361, and W4-1's #362. The re-run,
  40396621692, passed every job and every step. The one check of the
  brief's list that `rehearse.yml` does not run is
  `go install ...@latest`, and the record says so.
- **The plan's row** in `docs/INDEX.md` says the plan is done and links
  the record. The plan's own status line and its train closeout are the
  plan orchestrator's, and this item does not touch them.
