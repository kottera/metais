# ADR-013 — W3-10: release.sh refuses a draft whose target's workflows are not main's (D7 item 3)

**2026-10-24, PLAN-2026-10-metais-public W3-10.** Written at the train's
close, 2026-10-26, by W4-3: the change wrote no entry, and this one is
drawn from its merged pull request, #335 (merged as `723e3c16`), and the
Wave 3 closeout. It was made in Ed's release act W3-OOB4, under the item's
address.

rc.1's release run 40123046882 was approved, and its push of the tag was
refused: GitHub counts a ref created at a commit whose workflows differ
from the default branch's as a workflow change, which no `GITHUB_TOKEN`
may make. Nothing was pushed or published. By Ed's choice, a new
candidate and an early refusal with no new credential: `release.sh
prepare` compares `.github/workflows` at the draft's target with `main`'s
and refuses a mismatch before anything is pushed, dry run included,
naming the fix. `docs/dev/release.md` says to cut at `main`'s tip and land
no workflow change between the draft and the release act, Dependabot's
included. `TestReleaseScript` covers a target with other workflows,
refused, and a target behind `main` with `main`'s workflows, released.
rc.1 was retired, and rc.2 was cut after this merged.
