# ADR-010 — W2-1: forms and producers

**2026-10-21, PLAN-2026-10-metais-render-seams W2-1**, Amendment A7 items
4, 5 and 9. A planned file is expanded by its declaration's form and
produced by the producer that form sets, and no phase after planning
asks a file which form it came from.

- **Item 4.** `Form` (`DefaultPath`, `Expand`) is chosen by `formOf`'s
  `switch` over `manifest.OutputForm`, in `internal/render/form.go`. The
  forms are `form_template.go`, `form_asset.go`, `form_mirror.go` and
  `form_container.go`. `Planner` keeps `when`, `for_each`, the path's
  rendering or default and the `outputs` limit, and container-depth,
  which it holds before a declaration's expressions are evaluated. A form
  records its files with `Planner.plan`, so a container is counted
  before its entries, as it was.
- **Item 5.** `Producer` (`Record`, `Reach`, `Produce`, `Open`, `Method`)
  is chosen by `producerOf`'s `switch`, in `producer.go`. The producers
  are `producer_rendered.go`, `producer_copied.go`,
  `producer_assembled.go` and `producer_literal.go`. `File` has no `tool`
  flag. Its record methods, the templates parsed before rendering, the
  asset placement map, producing, measuring, `Open` and the zip method
  each ask the file's producer. Phase 4 calls each top-level file's
  `Produce`. A container's produces its entries in its order and then
  measures itself. The `output-size` findings of top-level containers
  are still reported after every file has been produced. Each producer
  measures its file's size and digest in phase 4, except `copied`, whose
  digest is still taken on the asset's first whole read. A profile's
  mimetype keeps its container's declaration without the id, as it
  keeps its pointer, so its record names no output.
- **Item 10, one goroutine.** A6 item 1's pipe is `assembled.Open`, the
  only `go` statement in `internal/render`. A container nested in
  another is still written in place, inside its parent's zip, and never
  through a pipe of its own. `TestAssembledContainer`, in that
  producer's file, drives the pipe under `-race`. It reads an epub with
  the profile's mimetype, a rendered entry, an asset and a nested
  container, compares the bytes with the measured digest, and closes a
  reader early.

The relocation is its own commit, before the rewrite.
`TestRenderCommitAbortsAFailedWrite` gives its two hand-built files the
`rendered` producer.
