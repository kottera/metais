# ADR-010 — W6-2: pack's benchmark

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W6-2.** D15 item 1's `pack`
benchmark is `BenchmarkPack` in `internal/cli`, with `b.ReportAllocs()` and
`cpu-ns/op`. It runs pack in-process, its check and test included, over two
sources: the scaffold `init` writes, and `aut/pack`'s source directory.
The §4.1 example joins them in Wave 7. `TestBenchPack` holds it to the work
it times in every `go test`. `internal/cli/testdata/bench/baseline.txt` was
rerun whole on linux/amd64 to add it, as a sandbox figure, not a budget.
