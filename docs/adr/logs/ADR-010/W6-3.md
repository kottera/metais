# ADR-010 — W6-3: the wave tier runs the SC7 reach over every vector

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W6-3.** This adds one step to the
wave tier that Amendment A5 item 1 defines: ADR-009 D16 item 1's
every-command reach over every vector. The ruling is R-W6-3 (plan
orchestrator, 2026-10-20), which extends A5.

Over the 397-vector tree, the full SC7 harness with its reach ran 28,908
tool processes. With the non-race test binary at `-test.parallel` 16 on a
shared 16-core darwin/arm64 host, it took 458 s of wall time and 1,107 s of
CPU time. Under the gate's shape, `go test -race -count=1 -p 5 -parallel 5
./internal/conformance` timed out at 10m0s inside `TestHostIsolation`.
`-timeout` may not be raised, and the share may not be enlarged.

So the reach runs only with `-conformance.reach`. `gate.sh wave` runs it as
one step per group of at most 25 vectors of one area
(`METAIS_GATE_REACH_CHUNK`). Each step is its own `go test -race -run
'^TestHostIsolation$/^<area>$/^(<slugs>)$'` invocation, under the existing
timeout and share. A group that runs fewer vectors than it selects fails.
The largest area as one invocation, `sec`, 32 vectors and 3,501 processes,
took 232 s.

Every `go test` keeps proving that the harness catches leaks. It runs
`TestHostIsolation` over every vector with the release build, carrying
environment A's further variables and decoys, both path modes, checks 1 to
4 and the crash check. It also runs the reach over every canary's probe
(`TestHostIsolationCanaries`) and in `TestHarnessReach`.
