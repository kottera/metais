# ADR-010 — W6-7: the benchmarks of D15 item 1, with committed baselines

**2026-10-19, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W6-7.** D15 item 1's benchmarks
exist, each with `b.ReportAllocs()`, except `pack`'s, which is W6-2's,
over the §4.1 example, which is Wave 7's:

- Liquid parse and render over three fixtures in
  `internal/liquid/testdata/bench/`: a 10,000-row table through
  `markdown_table`, a 500-page site over a collection through `render`
  partials, and conditionals nested 24 deep. They are parsed and rendered
  in `internal/template`'s dialect, under ADR-009's default limits;
- JSON, YAML and CSV parsing of a 10 MiB input, in `internal/jsonx`,
  `internal/yamlx` and `internal/inputs/csv`;
- schema validation of a 10,000-item collection, in `internal/schema`;
- zip reading of a package of 10,000 entries, in `internal/zipfmt`;
- `inspect` of a packed package of 10,000 entries, whole-package
  verification included, and `validate` of a 10,000-item collection read
  from a data directory, in `internal/cli`, run in-process.

Each of D15 item 2's three CPU budgets maps to one of them, which reports
`cpu-ns/op`: the table to `internal/liquid`'s `BenchmarkRender/table`,
`inspect` to `internal/cli`'s `BenchmarkInspect`, and validation to
`internal/cli`'s `BenchmarkValidate`. Each benchmarked package commits
`testdata/bench/baseline.txt`, the raw output of six counts, labelled as a
sandbox figure and not a budget. `docs/dev/benchmarks.md` holds the one
command that runs every benchmark, the benchstat comparison, and the
mapping. No budget is recorded here: plan §6's act measures them on real
hosts.

The two deterministic budgets of D15 item 2 were already tests:
`TestOpenReadsEveryByteOnce` in `internal/zipfmt` (W2-4.4) and
`internal/loader` (W3-1.6), and `TestReadLinesDecompressesOnceMore` in
`internal/cli` (W4-2.4).
