# ADR-010 — W2-2: stages, and a stage's growth charged to the output limits

**2026-10-21, PLAN-2026-10-metais-render-seams W2-2**, Amendment A7 items
6, 8 and 9. ADR-005 D13's post-render transforms are a table of stages,
and a stage's change to a file's length is charged.

- **Item 6.** `render.Stage` (`Name`, `Applies`, `Run`) and the slice
  `stages` in `stages.go`, in their normative order: `crlfStage` in
  `stage_crlf.go`, then `validateStage` in `stage_validate.go`, each in a
  file of its own. `runStages` runs every stage that applies, in order,
  and reports every finding of each; a file a stage left unrendered goes
  no further. Only the rendered file's path through `produce` calls it,
  at the call site W1-1 gave it.
- **Item 8.** `Staging.SetBytes` is the only way a stage changes a file's
  bytes. It charges the change in length against `output-size` and
  `output-total` through `template.Renderer.ChargeChange`, on the meter
  that charged the file's rendered bytes, so a breach of the total also
  refuses every later output's first byte, as one in rendering does. A
  breach is the file's `limit.exceeded.output`, at its path, a
  container's entry's by its container's path and its own; the file did
  not render, and no later stage runs on it. `Staging` carries the
  renderer `Run` constructs, so `MapOrder`'s control still sees the only
  one. The vectors `sec/output-size-crlf` and `sec/output-total-crlf`
  prove it, each red on `main` at `598443a` before the change, and
  `TestStageGrowthCharged` proves it for a container's entry.
- **Item 9.** The stages are one slice in one file; nothing registers
  itself in `init`.

The relocation of the CRLF conversion and validation into their files is
its own commit, before the table's. `TestCRLF` moved with the conversion
to `stage_crlf_test.go`.
