**The sandbox's breaches are confirmed on real hosts.** Only
`loop-iterations` stays within 10 s, on three of the four targets. The
three memory breaches are the same on every target.

Ed ruled on these figures as Amendment A3. The CPU ceiling is 120 s, and
every figure above is within it. The memory ceiling holds at 1 GiB, and
the defaults that drive `grep-context`, `schema-work` and `output-total`
past it are lowered by the implementing item.
