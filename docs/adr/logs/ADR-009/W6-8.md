# ADR-009 — W6-8: the hostile corpus of D17 items 1 to 4, and the opening limits at their bounds

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W6-8.** Every case D17 items 1 to 4
names is a `security` vector, in the form of ADR-011 D16, or an earlier
vector stands for it. Each new case's `package.metais` is written by
`internal/vectorgen/corpus_test.go`, and `TestPackages` reproduces its
bytes. Each package carries one defect around a correct `.compiled/` and
correct digests, unless the defect is a digest. Each `open` step refuses
through every command the tool declares as opening a package, with the
same findings and nothing written anywhere. It runs under `bounds` of
500 ms of CPU and 64 MiB, D18 item 2's ceiling for a refusal from
metadata.

- **Item 1, entry names.** `pkg/name-parent`, `name-parent-nested`,
  `name-absolute`, `name-drive-backslash`, `name-drive-relative`,
  `name-unc`, `name-backslash-parent`, `name-nul`, `name-empty`,
  `name-dot`, `name-empty-segment`, `name-segment-length`,
  `name-reserved`, `name-reserved-extension`, `name-reserved-port`,
  `name-stream`, `name-asterisk`, `name-trailing-dot`,
  `name-trailing-space`, `name-control`, `name-git-upper`,
  `name-git-joiner` and `name-git-short`. `sec/path-length-past` and
  `sec/path-depth-past` are the names over the limits. Earlier vectors
  stand for invalid UTF-8 (`pkg/name-encoding`), a name ending in `/`
  (`pkg/name-directory-entry`), `.git` (`pkg/name-not-portable`) and an
  unknown root (`pkg/unknown-top-level`).
- **Item 2, collisions and types.** `pkg/name-case-collision-ascii`,
  `symlink-inside`, `symlink-outside`, `mode-fifo`,
  `mode-character-device`, `mode-block-device`, `mode-socket`,
  `directory-entry-mode`, `directory-entry-data`, `mode-setuid` and
  `mode-executable`. Earlier vectors stand for duplicates
  (`pkg/name-duplicate`) and `a` beside `a/b` (`pkg/name-file-directory`).
- **Item 3, container structure.** `pkg/overlap-quoted`,
  `size-more-data`, `size-less-data`, `local-name`, `local-method`,
  `local-compressed-size`, `local-uncompressed-size`, `gap`,
  `count-more`, `count-fewer`, `count-disk`, `zip64-end-record`,
  `zip64-extra-field`, `method-lzma`, `method-zstd`, `method-aes`,
  `truncated`, `deflate-truncated` and `crc-wrong`. `sec/entry-ratio-default`
  is the 1032:1 entry, and `sec/entry-size`, `sec/package-uncompressed`
  and `sec/entries-packed` are the declared sizes and the entry count past
  their limits. Earlier vectors stand for the plain overlap, the CRC
  difference, prepended and trailing bytes, the archive comment, two end
  records, the zip64 sentinel, the encryption bit, bzip2 and the data
  descriptor.
- **Item 4, digests and compiled forms.** `pkg/digest-not-listed`,
  `digest-no-entry` and `digest-entry-added`, and `sec/nested-archive`,
  whose asset is a zip of 32 central directory headers naming one local
  header of 32 MiB of zeros. That asset validates and renders under
  64 MiB. Earlier vectors stand for an entry changed after packing, a
  wrong package digest and the two compiled forms.

**Item 12's pairs** for `package-size`, `package-uncompressed`,
`entries`, `entry-size` and `entry-ratio` are each one package. A
`validate` step accepts it at a lowered limit, and an `open` step refuses
it one unit past that limit. `entry-ratio` is proved only above 1 MiB
(R2-147). `sec/entry-ratio-floor`, exactly 1 MiB of zeros, is accepted at
the default, and `sec/entry-ratio-default`, one byte more, is refused.
`path-length` and `path-depth` are proved at their defaults: 512 bytes and
32 segments are accepted, and one past each is refused.

A packed stored zip asset within the final 65,557 bytes of a package is
refused as a second end record (D5 item 2). So `sec/nested-archive`'s
asset is deflated, and it is not copied to an output. The conformance
runner's own scan inflates every zip a step writes, and that scan would
expand a copied bomb inside the runner.

`TestConformance`'s default run, as W5-NB3 measured it, is the compiled
test binary alone and not race-built, on a shared 12-core linux/amd64
host. It went from 13.5 s to 22.9 s of wall time at `-test.parallel` 12,
and from 16.7 s to 21.7 s at `-test.parallel` 4.
