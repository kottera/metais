# ADR-007 — W7-4: the log completed from the plan's wave verdicts — Wave 3's split-run scanner and `init`, and Wave 4's `check`

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W7-4.** Plan §9 to §12 name ADR-007
in Waves 3 to 6. This log recorded each of those waves, but not three of
its items: W3-7 (D4), W3-8 (D1) and W4-4 (D2). This entry records them
from the plan's verdicts and the waves' closeouts
([wave 3](../../../briefs/brief-plan-2026-10-metais-v1-wave3.md),
[wave 4](../../../briefs/brief-plan-2026-10-metais-v1-wave4.md)).

**Wave 3.**

- **D4, the split-run scanner:** W3-7
  ([#52](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/52)) landed
  `internal/author/ooxml`, a hand-written lenient tokenizer over
  `liquid.Constructs`' spans. Both `template.ooxml.*` codes were listed W4
  in the unwired list.
- **D1, `init`:** W3-8 ([#51](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/51))
  landed `metais init` in `internal/author`. It writes D1 item 4's
  scaffold, held as golden bytes, keeps the empty-directory rule and has
  `--dry-run`. It uses `hostfs`'s write side unchanged. A suggested valid
  name is given only for `--name`, because one derived from `<dir>` would
  print part of the caller's path, which R1-011 forbids. Wave 6 pinned the
  scaffold through `check`, `test` and `pack` (W6-2).

**Wave 4, D2 to D4: `check`.** W4-4
([#68](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/68)) landed every D2 step,
in order, with `report.step_skipped`. It wires the cross-reference and the
OOXML scan. Chapter 13 states it as MTS-AUT-001 to 023, with 18 vectors.
The `template.xref.*` and `template.ooxml.*` codes left the unwired list,
except two that moved to Wave 5. Its OOXML CPU-ratio test failed once
under load on linux in Wave 3 and did not reproduce on darwin/arm64, for
the producer or the verifier.

**Wave 6** recorded its items here, and left one gap for Wave 7. `check`
accepts a literal output path that `render` refuses, such as
`../escape.txt`. The Wave 7 brief rules, under the stricter-rule
convention, that `check` refuses it. That is Wave 7's own item.
