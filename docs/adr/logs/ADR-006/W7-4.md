# ADR-006 — W7-4: the log completed from the plan's wave verdicts — Wave 1's finding model and Wave 3's linear findings

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W7-4.** Plan §7 to §12 name ADR-006
in Waves 1, 2, 4 and 6, and the work of Wave 3's W3-9 is this ADR's too.

record Wave 1, where most of this ADR landed, or W3-9. This entry records
them from the plan's verdicts and the waves' closeouts
([wave 1](../../../briefs/brief-plan-2026-10-metais-v1-wave1.md),

**Wave 1: D1 to D5 and D8 to D13.** W1-3
([#13](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/13)) landed:

- `internal/finding`: the model, `New` from registered codes only, and
  `Report`, with D8's order, de-duplication, bounded heap and exact
  totals;
- `report.truncated`, the JSON and human encodings of D10 and D11, and
  `Exit` with roles;
- the `Pos` and `Positions` types of D13 item 2, and `internal/schemafail`'s
  failure tree type;
- `spec/findings/registry.json`, registering exactly `limit.invalid` and
  `report.truncated`, with `finding.schema.json` and
  `finding-registry.schema.json` under `spec/schemas/1.0/` and their
  `internal/specdata` copies.

The ordering test was red against a report that keeps insertion order
(D13 item 5), and the cap keeps exact totals under a lowered `findings`
limit. W1-3's readings, each named in #13:

- past the cap, the report keeps the findings that tie with the last one
  listed, so that D8 items 3 and 6 stay exact;
- the registry's span enum adds `none`.

D10 item 4 puts `summary` in the envelope, which ADR-008 D10 did not
list. Wave 2's W2-1 settled it with the envelope `format, ok, findings,
summary, result, next`.

**Wave 2's rulings on the registry** (plan §8). Codes of class `usage` and
`internal` are proved by `internal/cli` tests, not by vectors, so
`TestFindingCodesCovered` exempts them, and rows 2 and 3 of the exit table
are proved the same way. A registered code that no vector produces yet is
listed in `internal/finding/testdata/unwired.txt`, with the wave expected
to wire it. That list only shrinks, and must be gone at Wave 7's close.

**Wave 3: linear findings.** W3-9
([#55](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/55)) landed `finding.Path`, a
token-structured pointer. Building, ordering and placing findings is now
linear on duplicate-heavy hostile input, with a library-level CPU-time
proof, and reports are byte-identical. It also fixed `Report.Add`'s panic
on three or more repeats. W3-9F
([#58](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/58)) made the
schema-validation path linear too, since `schemafail.Target.Locate` now
takes a `*finding.Path`. Wave 3 read D8 as de-duplicating repeats of one
name, which plan §9 lists for Ed to ratify.

**Wave 4: two codes.** W4-3 settled `limit.exceeded`'s single subject
with two new codes inside the registry model, `limit.exceeded.template`
and `limit.exceeded.output`. Ed ruled Amendment A1
([#78](https://github.com/kottera/metais/pull/78)), and Wave 7 carries
its work.
