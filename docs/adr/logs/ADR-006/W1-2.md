# ADR-006 — W1-2: the duplicate key compares the schema location

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-values-and-rules W1-2.** ADR-003
Amendment A2 item 8 amends D8 item 3: two findings are duplicates only when
their `schema.entry` and `schema.pointer` are equal too. `internal/finding`'s
duplicate key now holds the schema's entry and pointer beside code, subject,
origin, file, argument, pointer and parameters, and MTS-FND-003 says so in
`b80ed115`'s words. Two keywords at two locations that fail alike are two
findings: a table with one `minContains: 1` branch per criterion reports
every criterion it misses, where it reported one (the collapse #268
measured), and a member two `required` keywords require is reported once for
each. One keyword reached twice through a shared `$ref` ends at one authored
location (MTS-FND-005), so it stays one finding, the one whose
`keyword_location` sorts first. Since duplicates now share their schema
location, the report's group no longer holds the kept finding's entry and
pointer: only line and column can order two duplicates. `internal/inputs`'
`TestDuplicatesBySchemaLocation`, `internal/finding`'s
`TestReportDuplicates` and the vector `fnd/duplicate-key` prove it. No other
1.0 vector, golden or test expectation changed.
