# ADR-006 — W7-C7: `package.layout.readme` registered

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W7-C7.** ADR-001 Amendment A1 item 6
gives a packed package whose first entry is not the specification's
`README.md`, stored, a finding of its own. It is `package.layout.readme`,
an error of class `refused` about the package, located at `README.md`. Its
`reason` is `missing`, `not_first`, `compressed` or `altered`. Its entry is
in `internal/finding`'s package family, and `spec/findings/registry.json`
and chapter 12's catalog are generated from it. Its statements are
MTS-PKG-071 and MTS-PKG-072, and four vectors under `pkg/readme-*`
produce it.
