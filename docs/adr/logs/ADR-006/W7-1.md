# ADR-006 — W7-1: the manifest keyword codes no manifest can be given leave the registry

**2026-10-20, PLAN-2026-10-metais-v1 W7-1.** A1 item 1's fourteen codes,
and `manifest.any_of` and `manifest.one_of.ambiguous` by the wave 7
brief's ruling, are no longer registered. `manifestUnproducible` in
`internal/finding/schemacodes.go` names the sixteen, and the generated
`spec/findings/registry.json` and chapter 12's catalog no longer list
them. Chapter 12, §12.3, now says the `manifest.` family holds only the
codes the manifest schema of its revision can give, and why the 1.0 schema
gives none of these.

- **A1 item 4's tripwire is `TestManifestKeywordCodes`**, in
  `internal/finding`. It walks the manifest schema the binary embeds
  (`specdata.ManifestSchema10`) from its root, following its `$ref`s, and
  fails both ways:
  - when a keyword maps to a `manifest.*` code the registry lacks;
  - when the registry holds a `manifest.*` keyword code that no keyword
    of the schema maps to.

  So the registry holds exactly what the schema can produce.
  `TestManifestKeywordCodesControl` breaks it once per way. It was seen
  red against a registry without `manifest.pattern`, and against the
  registry before this change, which held all sixteen.
- **How the tripwire reads "maps to" (A1 item 4).** It applies D6's
  mapping where the keyword stands, so a keyword in a place whose failure
  the mapping never reports maps to no code:
  - the subschema of `not`, the `if` subschema and the subschema of
    `propertyNames` report nothing from inside them (D6 items 7 and 10),
    so the walk does not enter them, and the schema's one `anyOf`,
    beneath the name's `not`, needs no `manifest.any_of`;
  - a `oneOf` maps to `manifest.one_of`, and to
    `manifest.one_of.ambiguous` only when two of its branches could both
    match. The tripwire counts two branches exclusive when, through their
    `$ref`s, one requires a member the other, a closed object
    (`additionalProperties: false`, no `patternProperties`), does not
    allow. Every pair of the output's and the entry's four branches is so,
    since each requires its own source key and allows no other's.

  This reading is A1 item 2's "a keyword … has no failure to name", read
  per position: item 4's text holds it as written, since a keyword
  beneath a `not` maps to no code under D6. A minor that moves the
  `anyOf` out from beneath its `not`, or gives a `oneOf` branches that can
  overlap, makes the tripwire require the code, and
  `TestFindingCodesCovered` then requires its vector. The control proves
  both. W6-9's report of the schema was checked against the schema:
  the one `anyOf` is `/properties/name/not/anyOf`, and the two `oneOf`s
  are `/$defs/output/oneOf` and `/$defs/entry/oneOf`.
