# ADR-003 — W1-3: the ACR refuses a Level A or AA WCAG criterion answered Not Evaluated

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-values-and-rules W1-3.** The
accessibility conformance report example adopts Amendment A2 item 8's
per-row rule. Its WCAG 2.2 table's item schema gains an `if`, titled "A
Level A or AA criterion", over the 55 criterion numbers that
`templates/criteria/wcag-level.liquid` gives Level A or AA, and a `then`
whose `conformance` is `not` `Not Evaluated`. The rule is plain 2020-12
and adds no keyword and no specification text. A row it refuses is one
`input.not` finding at the row's `conformance` cell, with a `via` entry
naming the `then` and the `if`'s title, as A2's context and MTS-FND-006
say. On `main` at `a9d58851`, a WCAG table answering every criterion Not
Evaluated validated with no finding; now each of its 55 Level A and AA
rows is refused and none of its 31 Level AAA rows is.
`TestACRRequiredLevelsEvaluated` holds that, and so also holds the
schema's list of numbers equal to the level partial's: a number missing
from the `if` leaves a row unrefused, and a Level AAA number added to it
refuses one. The package's guide, `inputs/tables.md` and
`inputs/conformance.md` state the rule, since the last no longer lets a
WCAG Level A or AA criterion the evaluation's scope left out be Not
Evaluated.

**The Section 508 and EN 301 549 tables** (plan §4's last drafting
decision) are unchanged by this entry; see the pull request for the
decision and its reasoning.
