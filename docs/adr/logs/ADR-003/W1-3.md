# ADR-003 — W1-3: the ACR refuses a Level A or AA WCAG criterion answered Not Evaluated

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-values-and-rules W1-3.** The
accessibility conformance report example adopts Amendment A2 item 8's
per-row rule. Its WCAG 2.2 table's item schema gains an `if`, titled "A
Level A or AA criterion", over the 55 criterion numbers that
`templates/criteria/wcag-level.liquid` gives Level A or AA, and a `then`
whose `conformance` is `not` `Not Evaluated`. The rule is plain 2020-12
and adds no keyword and no specification text. A row it refuses is one
`input.not` finding at the row's `conformance` cell, with a `via` entry
naming the `then` and the `if`'s title, as A2's context and MTS-FND-006
say. On `main` at `a9d58851`, a WCAG table answering every criterion Not
Evaluated validated with no finding; now each of its 55 Level A and AA
rows is refused and none of its 31 Level AAA rows is.
`TestACRRequiredLevelsEvaluated` holds that, and so also holds the
schema's list of numbers equal to the level partial's: a number missing
from the `if` leaves a row unrefused, and a Level AAA number added to it
refuses one. The package's guide, `inputs/tables.md` and
`inputs/conformance.md` state the rule, since the last no longer lets a
WCAG Level A or AA criterion the evaluation's scope left out be Not
Evaluated.

**The Section 508 and EN 301 549 tables** take Ed Funnekotter's ruling
of 2026-10-23, option (c): a row there may be Not Evaluated only with
remarks saying why. Neither standard has levels, so the WCAG rule has no
condition to carry over. Refusing Not Evaluated in every row would leave
a third-party evaluator, the SC1 round's own case, no honest answer for a
criterion that applies but that it cannot assess, such as Section 508's
602 and 603 or EN 301 549's clause 12 on documentation and support. It
would have to evaluate what it cannot see, or call the criterion Not
Applicable falsely. Allowing it bare would leave the scope exclusion
`inputs/conformance.md` describes unenforced. So each table's item
schema gains an `if`, "Answered Not Evaluated" (conformance `const` Not
Evaluated, conformance `required`), and a `then`, "Says why it was not
evaluated", that requires `remarks` and gives it a pattern needing a
character other than a space, tab or line feed. A bare row is an
`input.required`, and one of spaces alone an `input.pattern`, at the
row's `remarks`, with a `via` naming the `if`'s title. The WCAG table
takes the same rule too, beside its Level A and AA rule in an `allOf`.
Only a Level AAA row can still be Not Evaluated there, and it now says
why as well, so one rule holds in every table. The guide, `tables.md`
and `conformance.md` say so. `TestACRNotEvaluatedSaysWhy` holds the rule
in all three tables, and on the schemas of `a9d58851` it fails with no
finding.
