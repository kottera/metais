# ADR-003 — W1-6: `uniqueKeys` evaluated, for inputs and outputs (Amendment A2)

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-values-and-rules W1-6.** Amendment A2's
`uniqueKeys` is built, specified and proved by vectors: chapter 5 §5.4 with
MTS-DAT-015, MTS-DAT-017 and MTS-DAT-018, MTS-DAT-004's new list, §5.4's
example, closing paragraph and note, chapter 4 §4.1.2's list, chapter 6
MTS-SUP-029 and chapter 12 §12.3's `uniqueKeys` row and MTS-FND-004, each as
`b80ed115` wrote it.

- **Item 19, the vocabulary spike (R-VR-6).** `internal/schema.NewCompiler`
  registers a rules vocabulary with `Compiler.RegisterVocabulary` and switches
  on `Compiler.AssertVocabs`, so the keyword applies to schemas that declare
  no `$vocabulary`. With the switch on and the vocabulary registered, the
  whole module's tests passed unchanged: every conformance vector, the
  JSON-Schema-Test-Suite run under `internal/schema/testdata` with its
  exclusions as they were, and the manifest schema's own compile in
  `internal/loader`. The fallback, the evaluator's annotation output read by
  a post-pass, is not built. No module dependency is added.
- **Item 14.** The pre-check holds `uniqueKeys` to MTS-DAT-015's grammar and
  refuses one that breaks it with `schema.invalid` naming the keyword, at
  open, so the vocabulary carries no meta-schema of its own.
- **Items 11 and 12.** Each item whose key repeats is one finding naming the
  first item with the key, at its value at the pointer when the keyword has
  one and at the item when it has several, the first item's same place in
  `related`.
- **Item 18.** A key is encoded once per item, numbers by their mathematical
  value and objects whatever their members' order, and looked up once, so
  the evaluation is linear in the items; `TestUniqueKeysLinear` holds it to a
  CPU time at the size a pairwise check fails.
- **`coverage` stays refused** as an unknown keyword until W1-7 builds it in
  the same vocabulary (`TestCoverageUnknown`).

Vectors: `dat/unique-keys`, `dat/unique-keys-grammar`,
`sup/unique-keys-partial` and `trn/output-unique-keys`.
