# ADR-003 — W2-3: check refuses a rule no table can satisfy and warns on a key no row can hold (Amendment A2)

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-values-and-rules W2-3.** Amendment A2's
drafting decision item 15 is built, specified and proved by vectors: chapter
13 §13.1 step 5's MTS-AUT-081, MTS-AUT-082 and MTS-AUT-083, each as
`b80ed115` wrote it, at the end of step 5. The prose section "Rules across
the rows of a table" above them is W2-6's.

- **Item 15, `schema.unsatisfiable`.** `check` resolves a `coverage`'s key
  against the array's item schema, token by token as MTS-AUT-013 resolves a
  property segment, and reports each member of `values` that every schema
  the key reaches refuses, at the member. It reports a `coverage` with more
  `values` than the `maxItems` of the object holding it, at `values`. The
  registry entry's statements are MTS-AUT-008, MTS-AUT-081 and MTS-AUT-082,
  and its message and hint name each of the three causes in words.
- **Item 15, `schema.key_not_declared`.** Each key pointer of a
  `uniqueKeys` or a `coverage` with a token no alternative of the item
  schema declares is a warning at the pointer. It is registered in
  `internal/finding` with MTS-AUT-083, a warning of class content with no
  parameters, as `schema.unsatisfiable` has none: no approved text states
  one, and the finding's location is the pointer itself.
- **Conformance.** `aut/check-coverage-values`,
  `aut/check-coverage-max-items` and `aut/check-key-not-declared`, each
  with its negative cases beside the reported ones: a value one
  alternative accepts, `values` exactly at `maxItems`, and a key one
  `anyOf` or `oneOf` alternative declares are not reported; a composite
  `uniqueKeys` with one undeclared pointer reports that pointer alone.
