# ADR-003 — W2-6: `metais help schemas`, `help schema`'s pointer, the skill and chapter 13's rules across rows (Amendment A2 item 10)

**2026-10-23, PLAN-2026-10-metais-values-and-rules W2-6.** The third and
fourth bullets of Amendment A2 item 10 are built; W2-5 built the others.

- **`metais help schemas`**, a new topic, teaches the dialect an author
  writes. It opens with chapter 13's example, laid out to the help's
  width, and the two rule lines `schema --fields` prints for it. It lists
  every keyword a schema may use by vocabulary, asserted and annotation
  (D3 items 3 and 4), with `uniqueKeys` and `coverage` as their own group;
  says that any other keyword but one beginning `x-` is refused, and that
  an `x-` keyword is carried and never interpreted (D3 item 5); states
  where a `$ref` may resolve (D5) and the pattern profile (D3 item 6);
  gives each asserted format with an example (D4); and teaches both rule
  keywords, what each is for and what it is not, and the check findings
  that guard them. The keyword list is held to the compiler's table
  (`schema.Keywords`, new) and the formats to `schema.Formats`, and each
  format's example is validated.
- **`metais help schema`** ends its detail with a pointer to the topic.
- **Chapter 13** §13.1, step 5, gains "Rules across the rows of a table",
  with its example and its Note, verbatim from `b80ed115`, immediately
  above MTS-AUT-081. Under ruling R-W2-a (plan orchestrator, 2026-10-23)
  the Note's "on the line of the array it constrains" reads as the tool
  builds item 10: the rule's line directly after its array's line.
- **The test.** `TestSchemasTopicExample` reads the example out of
  chapter 13, holds the topic's copy to the same JSON value, compiles it
  in a package that `check` finds no schema finding in, and holds
  `schema --fields` to its two rules directly beneath the array's line.
  The same schema with `uniqueKeys` misspelt is refused.
