# ADR-008 — W2-4, `metais schema --fields`, the compact field listing (Amendment A7)

**2026-10-22, PLAN-2026-10-metais-ux Wave 2.** Amendment A7 is
implemented.

- **The listing.** `internal/schema`'s `Walker.Fields` lists the fields of
  an input's document, or of the combined schema, as A7 items 2 to 5 say.
  It memoises what each schema declares at its own location, and expands
  the fields beneath each schema once, so a shared or recursive schema is
  named by `(fields as at <pointer>)` where it recurs. `metais schema`
  declares `--fields`, prints the listing as tab-separated lines, and
  carries it as `result.fields` of `metais.schema/1`, whose schema at its
  `$id` defines the second result shape. Without `--fields` nothing
  `schema` prints changed: `TestSchemaCommand` is unchanged and green.
- **The proofs.** `TestFieldsReachEveryDeclaration` holds every keyword of
  A7 item 4 to its line. `TestFieldsRecursive`, `TestFieldsSharedOnce`
  and `TestFieldsValueDepth` prove item 5 along its three axes at the
  smallest size that can fail it: one level of recursion, a chain of two
  shared links, and a depth of 1. With the once-only expansion disabled,
  the first two were red, the recursion listed until value-depth stopped
  it and the chain doubled at each link. With the depth check disabled,
  the third was red. `TestFieldsCuts` holds the cuts.
  `TestSchemaFields` lists the shipped examples' nested inputs: the
  accessibility report's `$ref`s into its text definitions, the static
  site's collection, and the DORA report's stage conditions, nested two
  deep. It checks that each JSON entry gives its text line exactly.
- **`inspect` and the skill.** `inspect`'s next step for each input is
  `metais schema <package> <input> --fields`, saying that it lists the
  input's fields (`TestInspectNextNamesFields`, red before the step
  changed). The skill's schema step names the listing before the whole
  schema and before `$defs` (`TestSkillSchemaStepNamesFields`, red on the
  skill's earlier text). The site's getting-started page runs the listing
  too.
- **Generated files.** The help goldens of `schema` and of the overview,
  whose one-line description of `schema` grew, were regenerated by
  `regenerate.sh`. No golden shrank. No conformance vector runs `schema`,
  and none changed.
