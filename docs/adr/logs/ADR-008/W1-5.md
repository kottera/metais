# ADR-008 — W1-5: A14, `metais version`'s maker line

**2026-10-22, PLAN-2026-10-metais-public W1-5.** A14 is implemented.
`metais version`, and so `metais --version`, ends its text output with
`METAIS is made by Kottera.`; `--json` prints the same `metais.version/1`
document as before.

- **The code.** `internal/cli`'s `runVersion` appends `makerLine` to the
  text output only. `internal/version` is unchanged.
- **The help.** `version`'s `Prints` says its output is three lines, the
  last saying who makes METAIS, without naming the maker. The help goldens
  `command-version.txt`, `command-help.json` and `help.json` change in that
  sentence alone.
- **The tests.** `TestVersion` pins the three lines for `version` and
  `--version`, and the document's bytes for `version --json`,
  `--version --json` and `--json --version`. Its text half was seen red
  against the build before the change. `TestVendorNeutral` holds every
  help output with a golden, the agent skill included, to naming neither
  Kottera nor tera, and `metais version` to naming its maker on its last
  line only.
