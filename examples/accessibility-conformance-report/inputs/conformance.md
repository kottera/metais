# Conformance levels

Every row of a table gives the product one of five conformance levels for
its criterion. Choose the level the evidence supports, and say what the
evidence is in the row's remarks.

- **Supports**: the product meets the criterion, and no defect against it
  is known. A product that meets it through an equivalent alternative, such
  as a second way of doing the same task that does meet it, supports it,
  and the remarks name the alternative.
- **Partially Supports**: the product meets the criterion in some of its
  functions and not in others. The remarks name the functions that do not.
- **Does Not Support**: the product does not meet the criterion in most of
  its functions. The remarks say what fails.
- **Not Applicable**: the criterion does not concern this product, such as
  a captions criterion for a product with no video. The remarks say why.
- **Not Evaluated**: the product was not evaluated against the criterion.
  Use it for a Level AAA criterion the evaluation chose not to cover, and
  for nothing that a buyer's standard requires: a report that leaves a
  required criterion unevaluated does not show conformance with it. A
  WCAG 2.2 criterion at Level A or AA is never Not Evaluated, and
  validation refuses it: evaluate each one before writing the report. Any
  other criterion the evaluation's scope left out, such as a Revised
  Section 508 or EN 301 549 criterion about documentation or support
  services an evaluator outside the vendor cannot see, is still Not
  Evaluated, never Not Applicable when it concerns the product. Its
  remarks say why it was not evaluated, and validation refuses a Not
  Evaluated row without them.

When in doubt between two levels, choose the lower, and explain in the
remarks. A buyer relies on a report as a statement of fact about the
product.
