# Sources and their terms

This package reports against three published standards, and it copies
none of their text. Every text in it is the METAIS project's own, written
for it: the manifest and its guide, the input schemas and meaning
documents, the templates, the criterion names and summaries of the
reference files, the definitions of the conformance levels, the JSON
report's schema, and the examples' data. The example's product, vendor
and findings are invented, and `example.com` is a domain reserved for
documentation by RFC 2606.

All of it is under the project's licence, the Apache License 2.0, as the
manifest's `license` says, with the repository's `NOTICE`.

No text of the Information Technology Industry Council's Voluntary Product
Accessibility Template (VPAT) is used: not its instructions, its
definitions of the conformance levels, its table headings or its layout.
The five conformance levels are named as PRD-001 §4.1 of the METAIS
project names them, and defined here in the project's own words.

Each source below is named with its publisher, where it is published,
what this package takes from it, the terms it is published under, and the
verdict of the METAIS project's terms check (plan §6, act W7-A1) on how
its text may be reused here.

## Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2

- **Publisher:** World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), W3C Recommendation of 5 October 2023,
  as updated 12 December 2024, <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>.
- **What this package takes:** each success criterion's number, its name
  and its conformance level, as identifiers; references/wcag-2.2.md
  summarizes what each criterion asks in the project's own words.
