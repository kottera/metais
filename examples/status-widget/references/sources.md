# Sources and their terms

Everything in this package — the manifest and its guide, the status
schema, the template and the examples — is written in the METAIS project's
own words for this package. It reproduces, adapts or quotes no third-party
text, so no third party's reuse terms apply to it.

The package is licensed with the rest of the repository, under the Apache
License 2.0, as its manifest's `license` says.

The four states — operational, degraded, outage and maintenance — are the
everyday vocabulary of service status pages, chosen here as words, not
taken from any one publisher's text or schema.
