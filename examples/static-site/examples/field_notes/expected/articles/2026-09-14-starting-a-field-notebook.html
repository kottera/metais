Starting a field notebook

14 September 2026 · notebooks, method

A field notebook is a record you can trust later, written at the moment you saw something. It does not need batteries, it does not mind rain, and nobody has ever lost an afternoon's notes to a software update.

The one rule

Write the date and the place first, every time, before anything else. An observation without a when and a where is a story, not a record.

What to carry

  • a notebook with numbered pages
  • a pencil, which writes on damp paper
  • a ruler marked in millimetres
