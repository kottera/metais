What to write down

2 October 2026 · method, weather

Every entry records the same five things, in the same order, so that a page read a year later answers its own questions.

date      2026-10-02
place     north meadow, by the gate
weather   overcast, light wind from the west
who       Sam, Ali
seen      two kestrels hunting the bank

The weather matters more than it seems: half of what you will not see is explained by it. Write it as you find it, and keep <b>tags</b> & symbols out of your head: this page shows them as you typed them.
